PLATFORM OVERVIEW

The All-in-One Safety Training & Compliance Solution

SafeLift 360 is a modern workplace safety training platform designed specifically for industrial, warehouse, and logistics organizations. By replacing passive slides with interactive 360° scenarios, employees gain hands-on hazard awareness while safety teams gain real-time visibility into workforce competency.

  • 🏢 Create a central company account
  • 👥 Manage workforce & employee profiles
  • 🎯 Assign interactive safety training
  • 📊 Track employee performance & scores
  • 📈 View detailed training results

CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Practical Workplace Safety

Comprehensive features built to improve learning retention, streamline administration, and enforce compliance.

🌐

Interactive 360° Training

Employees learn through an interactive 360° warehouse environment instead of reading traditional static training materials.

⚠️

Hazard Identification

Employees identify safety hazards, inspect loads, and check lifting routes through real-time interactive scenario hotspots.

📝

Assessments & Scoring

Employees answer practical multiple-choice questions and receive measurable, objective safety scores based on accuracy and attempts.

📈

Training Progress

Organizations can monitor individual employee training completion, time taken, score breakdowns, and attempt history.

📜

Digital Certificates

Employees who meet the required pass score receive downloadable, cryptographically verifiable PDF safety certificates.

🛡️

Administrative Oversight

Company administrators gain complete visibility to monitor employee training activity, pass rates, and compliance reporting.
